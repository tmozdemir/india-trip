The journey

Ten days through Delhi, Agra and Rajasthan, with a Great Rail Journeys tour manager looking after the group.

What to expect

This tour follows the Golden Triangle of Delhi, Agra and Jaipur, then reaches deeper into Rajasthan. There is a rickshaw ride through the Delhi bazaars, the Taj Mahal, the ancient Chand Baori stepwell, Amber Fort and two tiger safaris at Ranthambore. The last night is spent in a hilltop fort that once belonged to maharajas.

Pace: the tour company rates it "Comfortable Explorer". That means being happy with stairs, lifting a suitcase on and off transport, hills and uneven ground, and about an hour of walking with the group. Expect a few early starts and late nights, with rest time in between.
