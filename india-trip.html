⚡ Critical Rules — What You Must Do
🏥
Medical emergency — call BEFORE going to hospital
Call +44 (0)292 010 7777 within 24 hours if you need to stay in hospital, costs are likely to exceed £500, or you need to return home early. Failure to call can invalidate your claim.
🚔
Theft or loss — report to police within 24 hours
Get a written police report listing every item you are claiming for. Without this, the claim will not be paid. Also get an airline property irregularity report if luggage was in their care.
🧾
Keep all receipts
For any personal belongings claim you must provide original purchase receipts — price paid, where and when. No receipt = no claim. Photograph important receipts before you travel.
💊
Take your prescribed medication
No claim will be paid if it arises from you not taking medication prescribed for a pre-existing condition, or not following recommended treatment.
✈️
Check FCDO advice before and during travel
No cover if you travel to areas the FCDO advises against "all travel" or "all but essential travel." Check travel.gov.uk before departure and monitor during the trip.
✗ Key Exclusions to Know
Mobile phones & gadgets
Not covered unless you added Gadget cover (up to £1,500 if added).
Single items over £200
Personal belongings single item limit is £200. Anything more valuable needs separate cover.
Pre-existing conditions
Not covered unless you declared them, they were agreed, and you paid any additional premium.
Connecting flights mid-trip
Delays on flights between countries outside the UK are not covered unless Enhanced Trip Disruption was added.
Quad bikes & ATVs
No cover as rider or passenger — avoid these entirely in India.
Travel delay under 12hrs
Delays shorter than 12 hours are not covered as standard.
Alcohol / drug impairment
No cover for incidents where your judgement was seriously affected by alcohol or drugs.
Motorcycles / mopeds over 125cc
Not covered as rider or passenger. Auto-rickshaws are fine — privately hired motorbikes may not be.

📋 Other Important Notes
⚠️ Excess applies per person, per incident, per section claimed — check your policy schedule for the exact amount.
⚠️ Wear & tear deductions apply to belongings over 1 year old. New-for-old only on items under 12 months.
⚠️ India — no GHIC/EHIC applies. Your Admiral emergency medical cover is your only safety net — make sure the policy schedule confirms worldwide cover.
⚠️ Domestic flights (JAI→UDR, UDR→DEL) count as mid-trip connecting flights — delays on these are not covered unless you added Enhanced Trip Disruption.
⚠️ Valuables limit £200 per item. Camera, laptop, or any single item worth more than this is only covered up to £200 under standard cover.
⚠️ Health changes — you must tell Admiral if your health changes after the policy is issued, even with no trips booked.
